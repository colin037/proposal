There is something
I've been wanting to tell you from yesterday onwards... Maybe I don't know where
this will go... but I wanted to be honest
about how I feel. Even if this doesn't work out
I still want you to be a good friend of mine. According to you, a heart is just a hollow muscular organ that pumps blood through the circulatory system. It has four chambers, keeps us alive, and does its job every second without us even thinking about it. But I don't think that's all a heart is. I think a heart has a way of finding someone it wants to beat for. Someone who makes ordinary moments feel a little different. Someone who slowly becomes a little more special without us even realizing when it happened. Maybe that's what makes the heart so beautiful. It doesn't choose because it has to. It chooses because it feels. And I think being able to find a soul that your heart wants to beat for is one of the most beautiful blessings life can give us.
